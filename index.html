01 — About

Eight years of iOS, measured in crash rates and release cycles.

I'm a senior iOS engineer based in India. At Brainvire Infotech I lead iOS development for Nike ME, Under Armour ME and Sun & Sand Sports — retail apps serving 10K+ active users — where my work has cut crash rates by 30% and improved performance by 40% across the portfolio.

Most of my work is Swift: SwiftUI and UIKit, MVVM and Clean Swift, async/await and Combine. I care about the unglamorous parts — main-thread budget, caching, release automation — because that is where apps actually become reliable. I've reduced network calls by 25%, improved responsiveness by 40%, and cut deployment time by 50% with fastlane and Bitrise.

I also build AI-assisted developer tooling: local Ollama models and MCP tools inside Xcode (35% less boilerplate), a custom MCP server in Swift for worklog automation, and a self-hosted n8n pipeline on Docker and Oracle Cloud. Along the way I've mentored 5+ iOS developers on Swift, MVVM and VIPER.
